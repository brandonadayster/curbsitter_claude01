# Frontend and Brand Guidelines

## Design intent

Premium, local, calm, readable, and operationally credible. The visual system uses the existing deep-onyx direction without looking like a cryptocurrency dashboard.

## Tokens

```css
--color-bg: #070B14;
--color-surface: #0E1627;
--color-surface-2: #162238;
--color-border: rgba(255,255,255,.12);
--color-text: #F7FAFC;
--color-muted: #AAB5C6;
--color-cyan: #12D8F4;
--color-cyan-strong: #00BFD8;
--color-warm: #D7A64A;
--color-success: #2CCB7F;
--color-warning: #F0B44D;
--color-danger: #F26464;
```

These are starting tokens, not permission to add neon to every edge.

## Typography

- Use a highly legible modern sans serif available through the deployment platform.
- Body: 18px desktop where practical, never below 16px.
- Line height: 1.5-1.7.
- H1: clamp roughly 42-72px, short line length.
- Runner controls: 18-22px labels and obvious status text.
- Avoid thin font weights and all-caps paragraphs.

## Layout

- Max long-form line length about 70 characters.
- Generous vertical spacing.
- Primary CTA repeated after major decision sections but never spammed.
- Mobile form fields full width; no two-column name fields on narrow screens.
- Sticky mobile CTA is acceptable on marketing pages; it must not cover content.

## Components

- Address availability module.
- Plan cards with one recommended state, not five competing ribbons.
- Service-window explainer.
- Photo-proof timeline.
- Audience cards.
- Route-cell status map and legend.
- Four-step onboarding shell.
- Exception/status banner.
- Proof-photo card with metadata and accessible alt text.
- Runner task card and completion action.
- Admin queue/table with filters and bulk actions.

## Interaction

- Motion communicates state change, completion, and hierarchy.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No autoplay video with sound.
- No scroll-jacking.
- No endless testimonial marquees.
- Completion checkmarks are fine; fireworks are not.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 AA intent.
- 44x44px touch targets.
- Visible focus rings.
- Labels remain visible; placeholders are examples only.
- Error summary plus inline errors.
- Do not rely on color alone.
- Avoid low-contrast gray-on-onyx text.
- Maps have a text alternative and list view.

## Content voice

- Simple, reliable, local, direct.
- Slightly clever, never goofy.
- Explain the service in the first screen.
- Do not use "waste management" as if CurbSitter collects trash.
- Avoid "elite," "luxury," "revolutionary," and "AI-powered" in customer copy.
- Do not overpromise HOA compliance; say the service helps residents meet timing and storage rules.

## Approved core copy

- Trash day, handled.
- Bins out. Bins back. Photo-confirmed.
- Never miss trash day again.
- We roll your bins to the curb before pickup and return them after collection.

## Image rules

Use real or clearly licensed imagery showing Prescott-area homes, slopes, bins in context, seniors without condescension, local operators, and actual product screens. Never pass AI-generated people or testimonials off as customers.
