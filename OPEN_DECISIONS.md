# Open Decisions Before Public Launch

These are not permission for an agent to improvise. The owner must resolve them in `DECISION_REGISTER.md`.

1. Legal entity name, trade name, domain, email, and phone.
2. Founder offer, if any, and duration/eligibility. Standard prices are locked.
3. Exact active route cells, capacity, and start dates.
4. Complexity thresholds and adjustment ranges for long/steep/garage/gate/shared-bin properties.
5. Bulk Pickup Coordination provider-authorization procedure and separately quoted physical-placement limits.
6. Referral credit cap, expiration, tax/accounting treatment, and fraud policy.
7. Photo/access data retention period after account closure.
8. Worker employment/contractor model, background checks, driving standards, and insurance.
9. Customer service hours and response targets.
10. Final legal documents, ACH authorization language, and SMS/email consent language.
11. HOA pilot pricing and reporting format.
12. Exact pause/cancellation cutoff for already-generated service tasks.
