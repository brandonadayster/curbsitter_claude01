# Local SEO and Content Plan

## Position

CurbSitter is Prescott's local trash-bin rollout and return service, not a trash hauler. Every page must make that distinction clear.

## Foundation

- Create and verify a legitimate service-area Google Business Profile consistent with current Google rules.
- Use one accurate business name, phone, website, hours, and service-area representation everywhere.
- Publish real photos, service details, policies, and updates.
- Request reviews after a successful completed cycle; never buy, gate, fabricate, or suppress reviews.
- Respond to every review professionally and specifically.

## Core keyword themes

- trash bin rollout service Prescott AZ
- trash can to curb service Prescott
- bin return service Prescott
- trash day help for seniors Prescott
- snowbird trash can service Prescott
- vacation rental trash service Prescott
- HOA trash can compliance service Prescott

Use natural variants; do not stuff exact phrases.

## Page strategy

- Strong Prescott hub page.
- Segment pages for seniors/caregivers, snowbirds, rentals, and HOAs.
- Active route-cell/neighborhood pages only when they contain real unique information.
- Expansion pages may be waitlist pages but must be honest about availability.
- FAQ pages answer real operational questions.

## Local content pillars

1. Prescott collection and holiday schedule guidance, linked to official sources.
2. Steep-driveway and winter safety.
3. Javelina/wildlife prevention through correct timing and secure storage, without offering wildlife cleanup as the core service.
4. HOA timing/storage education with community-specific claims verified before publication.
5. Snowbird and remote property routines.
6. Vacation-rental turnover and missed-pickup prevention.
7. Route openings and service-area updates.

## Technical SEO

- Fast server-rendered pages.
- Unique titles/descriptions and one clear H1.
- Canonicals, XML sitemap, robots controls.
- LocalBusiness/Service/FAQ/Breadcrumb structured data only where truthful and eligible.
- Descriptive alt text and accessible maps.
- Internal linking from audience pages to pricing, how it works, service areas, and address check.
- No programmatic thin neighborhood pages.

## Conversion measurement

Track address-check start/result, waitlist join, plan selection, onboarding stage completion, checkout start/success, call/email click, HOA lead, referral share, and first completed cycle. Do not send addresses, gate details, names, phones, or proof-photo URLs to analytics.

## Initial content calendar

- Week 1: founder/local story and exactly what CurbSitter does.
- Week 2: how rollout/return proof works.
- Week 3: senior/caregiver safety guide.
- Week 4: route-cell opening post and direct-mail match.
- Month 2: snowbird, HOA, holiday, and wildlife timing topics.
- Month 3: real customer proof/reviews and first route lessons.
