# Marketing and Growth Plan

## Strategy

Market only where operations want density. Broad awareness without route fit is expensive applause.

## Priority channels

1. Google Business Profile and local search.
2. Facebook for older homeowners, caregivers, neighborhood groups, and retargeting.
3. Nextdoor for route-cell launches and neighbor referrals.
4. Direct mail/door hangers tightly matched to active collection routes.
5. HOA, property manager, realtor, senior-care, and home-watch partner outreach.
6. Instagram/Reels/YouTube Shorts as repurposed proof and education, not the primary lead engine.

## Message hierarchy

1. What it is: bins out and back.
2. Why it matters: safety, reliability, compliance, remote oversight.
3. Why CurbSitter: local, photo-confirmed, clear policies.
4. Availability: selected route cells.
5. Action: check the address.

## Referral program

- Give $20, Get $20.
- Credit only after the referred account completes the configured qualifying paid cycle.
- Self-referral, duplicate household/payment method, chargeback, and abuse checks.
- Pending, earned, applied, expired, and reversed states.
- Clear cap/expiration before launch.

## Route-opening campaign

1. Select the cell from operations data.
2. Publish/update the route page.
3. Contact waitlist first.
4. Send matched postcard/door hanger and local social posts.
5. Offer a time-limited founder benefit only if financially approved.
6. Ask every new customer for two neighbors.
7. Stop discounting when target contribution/capacity is reached.

## HOA sales offer

Lead with a 60-90 day pilot, selected residents/streets, resident opt-in, or preferred-provider model. Sell fewer bins left out, less board friction, help for older/seasonal residents, and one exception report. Do not promise zero violations.

## Content cadence

- 2 practical local posts/week.
- 1 proof/process post/week.
- 1 route or community update/week when truthful.
- 1 partner/HOA outreach block/week.
- Monthly email to customers/waitlist with collection changes, route news, and referral status.

## First 90 days

- Days 1-15: brand/profile/legal/operations foundation, target cells, landing pages, lead tracking.
- Days 16-35: address checker, waitlist, direct outreach, first pilot customers.
- Days 36-60: proof workflow, reviews, referral pilot, route-time measurement.
- Days 61-90: tighten pricing/capacity, add adjacent density, HOA pilots, publish real proof.

## Metrics

Lead-to-address-check, eligible-to-onboarding, onboarding completion, checkout success, first-cycle completion, CAC by cell/channel, referral share, review conversion, churn, and contribution by route cell.
