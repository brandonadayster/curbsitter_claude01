## 1. Consent is opt-in and separate

CurbSitter sends SMS **only** to customers who explicitly opt in. Consent to SMS
is collected separately from other agreements and is **not** a condition of
purchasing any service. Transactional service messages and marketing messages
require **separate** consent.

## 2. Message types

- **Transactional (service):** service confirmations (bins out / bins back),
  exception and hauler-delay alerts, schedule changes, and account/payment
  notices.
- **Marketing (separate opt-in only):** route-opening news and occasional offers.

## 3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your service schedule and events. **Message and
data rates may apply.** CurbSitter does not charge for the messages themselves.

## 4. Opt-out and help

- Reply **STOP** to any message to unsubscribe from that program; we will send one
  confirmation and then stop.
- Reply **HELP** for help, or contact support@curbsitter.com / (520) 225-9713.
- You can also manage SMS preferences in your dashboard. Opting out of SMS does
  not affect required **email** service notices.

## 5. Content limits (privacy)

CurbSitter will **never** send gate codes, garage codes, lockbox details, or other
sensitive access instructions by SMS.

## 6. Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on
your carrier and device.

## 7. Changes

We may update these SMS Terms; material changes will be communicated as required.

## 8. Eligibility

You must be the account holder or an authorized user of the mobile number you
enroll, and at least 18.
