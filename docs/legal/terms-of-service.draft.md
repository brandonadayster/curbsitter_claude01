## 1. What CurbSitter is

CurbSitter is a local trash-day concierge service in the Prescott, Arizona area.
We move a customer's household trash and recycling bins from their storage
location to the curb before scheduled collection, return the bins after
collection, and provide photo confirmation of each visit.

**CurbSitter is not** a waste hauler and does **not** collect, transport, or
dispose of trash or recycling; we do not replace your municipal or private
collection provider. We are not a property manager, security service, home
inspector, emergency responder, cleaning service, or handyman. We do not
guarantee that your hauler collects on any given day or that any homeowners'
association ("HOA") deems you compliant.

## 3. The service

### 3.1 Service windows, not appointment times
CurbSitter performs service within published **completion windows**, not at exact
appointment times. Rollout occurs during the evening-before window (default 5:00
p.m.–10:00 p.m. local). Return occurs after we confirm collection, targeted by
the end of pickup day, with a published fallback window if collection is delayed.
**We do not promise an exact arrival time.**

### 3.2 What's included
Covered subscriptions include evening-before rollout and post-collection return
for each covered collection day, timestamped photo confirmation each visit,
real-time exception reporting, holiday-schedule monitoring, normal property-
specific HOA timing instructions, a customer dashboard and service history, and a
minor javelina/wind reset discovered during a scheduled visit (uprighting a bin
and recovering a minor immediate spill).

### 3.3 What's not included
Standard service does not include waste transport, municipal collection charges,
junk hauling, hazardous-material handling, bin cleaning, widespread debris
cleanup, separate dispatches outside the scheduled route, security monitoring,
home inspection, or property management. These may be unavailable or separately
quoted.

### 3.4 Access and safety
You are responsible for providing accurate, current access information and for
keeping bins reasonably accessible. Our field staff may decline or stop work when
conditions are unsafe (including ice, lightning, aggressive animals, unstable
terrain, excessive weight, or any credible hazard). If we cannot safely or
reasonably complete a visit, we document the condition and notify you; no metric
penalizes a good-faith safety stop, and we are not liable for a visit we could
not safely perform.

### 3.5 Exceptions
If a visit cannot be completed as intended (for example, blocked access, missing
or overweight bin, or the hauler did not collect), we record a documented
exception, notify you, and follow our published recheck and resolution rules. A
delayed hauler is reported as a delay, not a completed return.

materials, and we do **not** guarantee that any provider accepts or collects the
items. Physical curb placement, where offered, is a separately reviewed and
quoted service subject to weight, access, terrain, staffing, and insurance
