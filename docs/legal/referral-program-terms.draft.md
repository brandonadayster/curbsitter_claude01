## 1. How it works

An existing customer or waitlist member ("Advocate") shares a personal referral
link. When a new customer ("Referred Customer") signs up using that link and
**completes their first paid collection cycle**, both the Advocate and the
Referred Customer receive a **$20 account credit**.

## 2. Credit is account credit, not cash

Credits:
- are applied only to a CurbSitter account and reduce future CurbSitter charges;
- are **not** cash, are **not** redeemable for cash, and are **not** transferable;
- have **no monthly cap** (owner-confirmed 2026-07-23); and
- accrue as **pending** and become spendable only after CurbSitter review and
  approval.

## 3. Qualifying event and timing

A referral qualifies only when the Referred Customer's **first paid collection
cycle is completed** (not merely signed up or charged). CurbSitter reviews each
qualifying referral before releasing credits.

## 5. Anti-fraud and eligibility

Credits are void and may be reversed if CurbSitter reasonably determines abuse or
ineligibility, including: self-referral; the same household, address, or payment
method on both sides; fake or duplicate accounts; chargebacks or unpaid balances;
or any attempt to game the program. CurbSitter's determination is final. Referral
states include pending, earned, applied, expired, and reversed.

## 6. Changes and termination

CurbSitter may modify or end the program at any time; changes do not retroactively
revoke credits already **earned and approved** except in cases of fraud or
reversal.
