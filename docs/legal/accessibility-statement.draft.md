## Our commitment

CurbSitter serves many older adults, and accessibility is a design requirement.
We aim to meet the intent of the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)
2.2, Level AA**, including:

- readable type (minimum 16px body text) and strong contrast;
- visible keyboard focus and full keyboard operability;
- labels that stay visible, with clear inline and summary error messages;
- respect for reduced-motion preferences;
- 44×44px minimum touch targets on primary and field controls; and
- text alternatives and list views for map content.

## Measures we take

- Automated accessibility checks (axe, WCAG 2.2 A/AA) run in our test suite on key
  public pages.
- Mobile and reduced-motion states are tested.
- `[Planned: periodic manual screen-reader review and third-party audit.]`

## Known limitations

The product is in active development, and some pages and flows are still being
audited with assistive technology. We are working to resolve known gaps.

## Conformance status

We consider the site **partially conformant** with WCAG 2.2 AA: most content
conforms, but some has not yet been fully verified with assistive technology.
