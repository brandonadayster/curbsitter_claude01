> account for the recurring quarterly plan amount I selected — **$159 (Home)** or

> correct any erroneous debit.
>
> I understand:
> - This authorization is for **recurring** debits that repeat **every three
>   months** on or about the renewal date until I cancel.

>   any change to the amount or date as required by the NACHA Rules.
> - I may **revoke** this authorization at any time by canceling in my dashboard
>   or contacting support@curbsitter.com, and revocation is effective for future
>   debits once CurbSitter has a reasonable opportunity to act on it (at least
>   `[3]` business days before the next scheduled debit).
> - I must be an authorized signer on the account and the information I provide is
>   accurate.
> - If a debit is returned for insufficient funds or other reasons, CurbSitter
>   may reattempt the debit `[up to 2 additional times]` as permitted by the NACHA
>   Rules, and my service may be paused or moved to another payment method.
> - I have the rights afforded by Regulation E for consumer electronic fund
>   transfers, including the right to dispute unauthorized transfers with my bank.
>
> I acknowledge I am agreeing to this authorization electronically and that it has
> the same effect as a signed paper authorization.
