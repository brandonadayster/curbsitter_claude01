CurbSitter serves many older customers and handles sensitive property and access
information. Protecting it is a core design requirement, not an afterthought.

---

## 1. Information we collect

**You provide:**
- Identity and contact: name, email, phone, and mailing/service address.
- Roles: payer, service recipient, and notification recipients (which may be
  different people).
- Property details: bins, collection provider and day(s), storage location, curb
  placement, and hazard notes.
- **Access instructions** (gate/garage/lockbox/key details) — treated as
  sensitive; see Section 5.
- Communication preferences and consent records.

**Collected automatically or generated by the service:**
- Service records: cycles, tasks, exceptions, and status history.
- **Proof photos** of bins and their placement, with timestamps.
- Limited device/usage and diagnostic data, and (only where permitted and
  operationally justified) a single location capture at task submission.
- Payment metadata from our processor (we do not receive full card/bank numbers).

**From third parties:**
- Address normalization/geocoding results from our maps provider.
- Payment status and fraud signals from Stripe.

We do **not** knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## 2. How we use information

- Provide, schedule, and prove the service, and handle exceptions.
- Verify serviceability and route fit before first service.
- Process payments and prevent fraud (including referral abuse checks).
- Communicate service updates; send marketing only with separate consent.
- Improve reliability and route economics using **aggregated or de-identified**
  operational data.
- Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use proof photos for
advertising or publish them publicly.

## 3. How we share information

- **Service providers (processors)** acting on our behalf under contract:
  hosting/database (Supabase), payments (Stripe), email (`[Resend/Postmark]`),
  SMS (Twilio, after consent), maps/geocoding (`[Mapbox/Google]`), and error
  monitoring (Sentry, with sensitive-data filtering).
- **Field staff** — limited to the assigned task and service window (least
  privilege).
- **Legal** — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.
- **Business transfer** — in a merger or asset sale, subject to this Policy.

## 4. Proof photos

We capture only what proves service or documents an agreed exception. We avoid
capturing doors, windows, interiors, people, and license plates when not needed,
and we do not use facial recognition. Photos are stored in **private** storage
and shared with you only via **short-lived signed links**. There is no public
gallery. See Section 7 for retention.

## 5. Access instructions (sensitive data)

Gate, garage, lockbox, and key details are:
- stored **separately** from ordinary property notes and **encrypted**;
- disclosed only to the **assigned field worker during the active service
  window**, with each disclosure audited;
- excluded from analytics, ordinary logs, error reports, and notifications; and
- **never** sent in plain SMS or email.

## 6. Communications and consent

Transactional email is the default channel for service messages. SMS is sent only
after explicit opt-in (see SMS Terms). Marketing email/SMS require separate
consent, which you can withdraw at any time (unsubscribe link or reply STOP). We
record the exact consent language version, channel, purpose, timestamp, and
source.

## 8. Your choices and rights

You may:
- access and update account and property information in your dashboard;
- request a copy or deletion of your personal information (subject to legal and
  operational limits, e.g., records we must retain);
- opt out of marketing at any time; and
- manage SMS via STOP/HELP.

To make a request, contact support@curbsitter.com. We will verify your identity
before acting on sensitive requests.

## 9. Security

We use private object storage with signed access, row-level security plus
server-side authorization, application-layer encryption for high-risk access
data, least-privilege staff access, audit logging of privileged actions, secure
transport, secure headers/CSP, rate limiting on public forms, and verified,
idempotent payment webhooks. No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee
absolute security.

## 10. Third-party links

Our site or messages may link to third parties (e.g., Stripe's billing portal)
governed by their own policies.

as required.
