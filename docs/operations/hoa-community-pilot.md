# HOA / Community Pilot — Pricing & Reporting — DRAFT

> Resolves OPEN_DECISIONS #11. Consistent with the Community & Portfolio custom-
> proposal model and the admin accounts view (HOA/portfolio grouping) already
> built.

## Offer architecture (from the playbook)

1. **60–90 day pilot** for selected streets/residents.
2. **Preferred provider** with resident opt-in (residents subscribe individually).
3. **HOA-funded** selected coverage or community-wide contract.
4. Monthly exception + completion report to the board.

Lead with fewer bins left out, help for older/seasonal residents, cleaner curb
appearance, and clear reporting. **Never promise zero violations** — the service
helps residents comply; it cannot control residents or haulers.

## Pricing model (draft — owner to set)

Community & Portfolio is custom-quoted. Recommended structure:

- **Resident opt-in (preferred provider):** residents pay standard Home/Complete
  pricing; no cost to the HOA. Optional community setup/coordination fee `[$__
  one-time]`.
- **HOA-funded selected residents:** per-home monthly `[= standard plan or a
  negotiated community rate for volume]`, billed to the HOA.
- **Community-wide contract:** volume rate `[$__/home/mo]` at `[__]`+ homes on a
  compatible schedule, `[term]`, with a route-density minimum.
- **Pilot:** `[discounted/founder rate or standard]` for the pilot window, clearly
  time-boxed and financially approved.

Do not stack discounts beyond what's approved; keep pricing configuration-driven.

## Reporting format (draft — monthly)

Board-facing report, per community, covering the period:

- Completed visits and proof rate (cycles with both rollout+return proof).
- Exceptions by category and property (bins blocked, hauler-missed, etc.).
- Missed-hauler-pickup instances (for the board to raise with the provider).
- Recurring trouble spots / recommendations.
- Roster of participating homes (opt-in) — **without** exposing individual access
  details or private photos to the board.

The KPI inputs already exist in `/admin/reports` and `/admin/accounts`; a
per-community export is a follow-on feature.

## Privacy guardrail

Community reports use aggregate/roster data only. Individual proof photos and
access instructions are never shared with the HOA. Resident-level detail is shared
only with that resident.
