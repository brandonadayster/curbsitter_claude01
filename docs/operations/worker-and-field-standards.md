## 1. Classification (owner + attorney decision)

CurbSitter field work (runners) can be structured as W-2 employees or 1099
independent contractors. This is a legal determination, not a preference.

- **Recommendation for launch:** `[ATTORNEY: advise. Note that Arizona uses
  common-law/economic-realities and ABC-style tests depending on the law at
  issue (wage, unemployment, workers' comp). Misclassification risk is real for
  scheduled, supervised, uniform-instruction route work.]`
- Whichever model: put it in writing (offer letter or contractor agreement),
  define scope, pay, and control consistent with the classification.

## 3. Driving standards

- Valid license and insurance at all times; report violations/suspensions.
- No phone use while driving; follow all traffic laws.
- Vehicle roadworthy; `[company / personal]` vehicle policy defined.
- Incident reporting is mandatory (see Incident Response Plan).

## 4. Insurance (broker to confirm — see Insurance Checklist)

- General liability; commercial auto and/or hired-and-non-owned auto if personal
  vehicles are used; workers' compensation (required in Arizona for employees);
  property-in-care/custody/control considerations for handling customer bins and
  bulk items; cyber/privacy.

## 5. Field conduct & safety standards

- **Least privilege:** runners see only the assigned task and access details, and
  only during the service window (enforced in software).
- **Safety-first:** stop work for ice, lightning, wildfire restrictions,
  aggressive animals, unsafe traffic, unstable terrain, excessive weight, or any
  credible hazard. No metric penalizes a good-faith safety stop.
- **Photo discipline:** capture only what proves service or an agreed exception;
  avoid people, interiors, windows, and license plates when not needed.
- **Access-secret handling:** never write down or share codes outside the app;
  reveal only in-app during the window.
- **Professionalism:** uniform/ID `[per policy]`; respectful, no entry into
  occupied living space; no unrelated services.
