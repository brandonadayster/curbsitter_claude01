# Founder / Launch Offer Policy — DRAFT (optional)

> Resolves OPEN_DECISIONS #2. A founder offer is **optional** and is the owner's
> decision. It must **not** alter the locked standard prices (D-004) — it can only
> be a time-boxed, clearly disclosed benefit layered on top.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)

- Standard prices stay locked: Home $59/$159, Complete $89/$240, One-Time $39.
- Any offer is **time-limited** and **financially approved** before launch.
- No stacked discounts and no punitive card surcharges (D-004 / retired-decisions).
- Disclose terms clearly; honor them; don't extend informally.
- Configuration-driven if implemented (do not hardcode in pages).

## Template options (pick zero or one)

1. **Founding-member benefit (recommended, margin-safe):**
   - "First `[N]` homes on a route get `[a small perk]`" — e.g., a founder badge,
     priority support, or `[one included One-Time Trash Day credit]` — rather than
     a price cut.
2. **Time-boxed intro credit:**
   - "`[$__]` account credit after your first completed paid cycle, for signups
     before `[DATE]`." (Reuses the credit system; margin-controlled.)
3. **Referral emphasis instead of discount:**
   - Lean on the existing Give $20/Get $20 program rather than a founder discount.

## If an offer is run

- Define: eligibility, exact benefit, start/end dates, and per-account limit.
- Set it in config; show it truthfully on the site (no fake scarcity/counts).
- Track redemption and its margin impact in reports.
- Stop when the target contribution/capacity is reached.

## Owner decision

- [ ] No founder offer at launch, **or**
- [ ] Offer chosen: `[which template, exact terms, dates]`.
